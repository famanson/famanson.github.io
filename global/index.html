Hello CNNers!

We are Global CNNers, and we've come around to say hi.

We are not different from you. We too started out - way back in the day - as students at this school.
Then we left for a new adventure in distant lands, clueless and innocent (maybe more clueless than innocent)
We had successes and we had failures. More importantly, we learnt from them - and we felt compelled to share this knowledge.
We want to pass the torch on to the next generation. A bunch of us got together for the first Conference. 6 years ago.
