Team Structure

The project may seem to be closely tied to SVUK, but we are granted total independence to operate and organise ourselves.

Since our skill set is conveniently split, we have decided that the core team would comprise of two smaller teams:

- A front-end client-side team will be led by Hai: design the main website front-end and potentially smartphone application(s) if we have enough manpower

- A back-end server-side team led by Son: deal with everything running behind the scenes like back-end services, database management etc.

Both Son and Hai will share a project management role and handle administration and organisation tasks.

Rewards and Benefits

We are sorry that we won’t be able to pay you :-( We know unpaid work sucks (been there, done that) so we’d understand if you give it a pass. However, benefits for joining Project Spring Roll comes in many other forms.

For students

- Exposure: joining us means that you get exposed to a lot of new technologies, routines, methods and tools that pros use in their daily work

- Recognition: by the end of the project, if you do well, we can recommend you to be considered for many award certificates and prizes from them.

- Impression: you will work alongside top engineers around town, guaranteed. If you impress them enough, your CV may find a shortcut to their HR or manager.

- Improvement: speaking of CV... Well, you know what to do with your CV.

For professionals

- Learning: our technology stack includes some of the hottest stuff in town, and we will use a more of a startup-type, less conventional approach to development.

- Networking: the number of Vietnamese software engineers in the UK is poor, and the heavily finance-oriented Vietpro does not help. This is probably the best chance that we can see each other and collaborate to build something great!

- Refreshing: what is a better way to spend a weekend than gathering with your friends, pop a beer and do an awesome side project together? (Answer: there is none)

- Free pizza: yep, there will be free pizzas and soft drinks in our gatherings.
