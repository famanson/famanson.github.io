Project Spring Roll

This project, bearing our namesake, is our debut product. We aim to create a whole new SVUK Marketplace with a complete end-to-end web system. We will build everything from scratch, and we need your help to make it happen.

You can either go to our Indiegogo campaign here to give us some financial support or keep scrolling down if you know a thing or two about coding and want to get your hands dirty!

About Us

We are both Worcester College, Oxford University alumni. Hai (right) is now an Android developer at Google UK and Son (left) looks after the webshop back-end applications at Ocado.

We are very close friends, sharing not only many favourite video games but also the devotion to improve our Vietnamese community in the UK.

About You

We are going to build the Marketplace in our own time, and that is definitely not a two-man job! We want you to join us in our core dev team on this journey (it is dangerous to go alone after all). Ideally, you ...

... should be either a software engineer, an IT professional, or a student with very good coding aptitude.

... don't mind taking new challenges head-on, are willing to learn and adapt to new, trending technologies

... have a burning desire to make a change for the better of the Vietnamese community in the UK.

Team Structure

The core team would be split into two smaller teams:

- A front-end client-side team will be led by Hai: design the main website front-end and potentially smartphone application(s) if we have enough manpower

- A back-end server-side team led by Son: deal with everything running behind the scenes like back-end services, database management etc.

Both Son and Hai will share a project management role and handle adminstration and organisation tasks.
