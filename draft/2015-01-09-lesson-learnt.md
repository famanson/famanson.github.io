... is how to prepare for a pitch

Being able to communicate with others well and to explain an idea clearly are not always stated in software engineer's description. But as socially awkward as some of us might be, communication, ranging from answering questions, seeking help, small talks or big presentations, is a big part of the job. Software engineers talking to each other about what they are working on is actually the easy part since we can relate to each other more easily.

The more difficult ones are where you need to help the non-tech crowd grasp what you are trying to say. I had a sudden realisation a while back that this side of my game really sucks when I was called into a meeting with an Ocado Tech head and a Morrisons exec to explain a bit of code that we got wrong* affecting multiple teams in the business and discuss what to do next. While I was mostly in that meeting as a point of reference to provide more technical insights on the problems, I completely misunderstood what was really relevant.
