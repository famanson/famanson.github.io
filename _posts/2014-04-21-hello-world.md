About damn time! Today, we announce the launch of [Team Spring Roll](https://famanson.github.io/spring-roll) and the beginning of our first project with the same name, [Project Spring Roll](https://www.indiegogo.com/projects/project-spring-roll) after a full month of preparation in stealth.

I came up with the idea a long time back, but never had the sufficient skills, determination, support or time to piece everything together until I pitched it to Hai in March. Since then we have been working very hard in our own time to draw up a proposal, team organisation, operation plans, writing the specification, and creating the Indiegogo campaign. In the process, we have developed a much better vision for both the team and the project with great input from SVUK and Vietpro (the incredible Thao and her husband Aaron in particular). Thanks a lot, guys!

The public announcement today means not only the start of our fund-raising campaign, but also that:

* active development of the SVUK Marketplace website starts **now**
* you should expect a beta test in the next 6 months
* our first code workshop will also come up very soon

Please drop us a line at [ukspringroll@gmail.com](mailto:ukspringroll@gmail.com) if you wish to get to know more about us and the project. *You are very welcomed to attach a CV to your email* in case you'd like to join our team! We are looking forward to hearing from you.

If you want more regular updates, [our Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/TeamSpringRoll) is a more suitable place. This journal will be my own geeky corner where I share my personal experience and commentaries about Team Spring Roll's challenges and all the fun we have solving them.

That's it from me today. I really need to grab an Easter egg...
